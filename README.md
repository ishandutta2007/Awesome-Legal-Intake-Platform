# Awesome-Legal-Intake-Platform

## Top Legal Intake Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Legal Service Requests, Matter Intake, Workflow Automation, Legal Ops & Corporate Legal Management*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Legal Intake**. These systems help corporate legal departments and law firms capture, triage, route, and manage incoming legal service requests, matters, and related workflows.



**Examples** include Onit Legal Service Management, LawVu, Checkbox, SimpleLegal, ServiceNow Legal, Legal Tracker, Mitratech TAP Workflow, BRYTER, Josef, and Checkbox AI (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Specialized legal intake and enterprise legal management platforms are largely commercial. Open options exist mainly as general workflow tools, experimental CLM/intake projects, and broader open legal management systems. This section lists the strongest available open resources and is realistic about the gap.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Onit Legal Service Management](https://www.onit.com/)**  

  Enterprise legal workflow and intake platform with strong automation, matter management, and CLM capabilities (includes SimpleLegal in the broader suite).



- **[LawVu](https://lawvu.com/)**  

  Legal operations platform supporting intake, matter management, and collaboration for corporate legal teams.



- **[Checkbox](https://www.checkbox.ai/)**  

  No-code legal workflow and automation platform used for intake, decision trees, and legal service requests.



- **[SimpleLegal](https://www.simplelegal.com/)**  

  Legal spend and matter management solution (now part of the Onit ecosystem) focused on mid-market usability and e-billing.



- **[ServiceNow Legal](https://www.servicenow.com/)**  

  Legal service management capabilities built on the ServiceNow platform for intake, workflow, and enterprise service management.



- **[Legal Tracker (Thomson Reuters)](https://legaltracker.thomsonreuters.com/)**  

  Legal spend and matter management platform widely used for tracking outside counsel and related intake processes.



- **[Mitratech TAP Workflow](https://mitratech.com/)**  

  Workflow and intake automation solutions within the broader Mitratech enterprise legal management portfolio.



- **[BRYTER](https://bryter.com/)**  

  No-code automation platform popular with legal teams for building intake forms, decision logic, and self-service workflows.



- **[Josef](https://joseflegal.com/)**  

  Legal automation platform supporting intake and process automation for legal service delivery.



- **[Checkbox AI](https://www.checkbox.ai/)**  

  AI-enhanced legal workflow and intake automation capabilities within the Checkbox platform.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects

  Early-stage open-source efforts focused on request-to-repository workflows, intake, and contract lifecycle management.



- **[Open legal management systems (e.g., Iuris-Soft and similar)](https://github.com/)**  

  Community projects providing contacts, matters, litigation, document, and basic legal project management features.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Adapting general open workflow and form platforms for basic legal request intake.

- Experimenting with early open CLM/intake projects for controlled evaluation.

- Combining open ticketing systems with commercial legal platforms for hybrid intake.

- Accepting that enterprise legal intake, matter management, spend control, advanced no-code legal automation, and compliance-grade audit trails still favor commercial platforms (Onit, LawVu, Checkbox, Mitratech, ServiceNow Legal, Legal Tracker, BRYTER, etc.).

- Focusing open-source efforts on internal tooling and interoperability rather than replacing full legal operations suites.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Capture requests via open forms or ticketing → apply routing rules and decision trees → create matters in an open or commercial system → track approvals and documents → report on volume and cycle time. Suitable for smaller legal teams or specialized internal processes. Most corporate legal departments continue to rely on commercial legal intake and ELM platforms for scale, security, and vendor support.
